## Findings

- **T12-F1** (fixed, Task 18): the doubles server's name replaced the Games count; it now sits under the side's name.
- **T12-F2** (engine, backlog): after an odd-games Set score the serve ball is hidden for the rest of the match. A tier-0
  `tennis.set_summary` banks the set without advancing `state.serving` while the serve walk advances, so
  `serveContext().serveOrderKnown` stays false from then on (seen on the device in sets 2 and 3). The phone is honest (no
  ball, and points carry no `server`, ruling 234). The fix is the engine's: the summary advances the serve by the set's
  game parity (a fold epoch bump). The owner decides.
- **T12-F3** (fixed, Task 19, 78449e28): at 320 × 640 in nl a decided match's result card left the two final boards no
  room. A decided or ended match now scrolls the card and both halves, each at its own height.
- Minor copy: Set score's refusal says "a game in progress can't have that score" where it means the set; the long name
  on the serve button breaks mid-word; the shared "Another scorer since" reads unfinished (rewording offered, open).
