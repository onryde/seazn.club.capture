## Known limits

- **320 × 568.** The supported floor is **320 × 640**. At 320 × 568 the board's last rung (171 dp) still needs 49 dp more
  than it has (fix 6; `RUNG_AT`); it clips at its bottom and never covers the keys. At 320 × 626 and 640 it fits (jsdom).
  Android phones 320 dp wide are 640 dp tall or more in practice. No scroll or compact mode exists below that.
- The shared `PadContent` header-to-board gap (38 dp) is left for every sport.
- A lineup saved while the pad is open is read only on the next open: the bootstrap is read once per app process
  (`once` in `scoringRunnerReads.ts`), no ledger event nudges it. A scorer who scans before lineups exist and taps Start is
  in over totals for that innings. Lineups should be saved before anyone starts the match.
- A rain tap at a break, then Start innings, never says "Innings 2 started": a pick has no timestamp, so the last tap
  cannot be ordered (M4). No score effect.
- The Interruption has no end control on the phone (§4.8); the organiser ends it.
- The phone's last-tap line after an organiser's multi-void names the latest event only.
- A Players sheet once swallowed five taps after a super over side's first ball (run 6); not reproduced.
