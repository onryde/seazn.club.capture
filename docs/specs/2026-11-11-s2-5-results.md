## The 2022 World Cup final, replayed (2026-11-11)

The owner asked whether rotating and capped subs had been tested. The answer was "partly", so the controller had the
final replayed through the phone's own hook (`useFootballMatch`, the real runner, queue, sync and fold over the fake
server, which judges every append with the engine): 27 tests, 12 `it.fails` for findings, on `test/s2-5-replay`
(`d9592514`), cherry-picked as `a25a8163`. The replay data is `footballReplay.wc2022.ts`; the replay is
`useFootballMatch.replay.test.tsx`; the report is `replay-wc2022-report.md`. One error in the brief was the
controller's: France kicked first in the real shootout.

## Device findings and what became of them

  sent. Verdict: not an app defect. **F3** (taps lost or misplaced while a sheet animates, a stray 1 s Correct): the same
  class, stray touches near the top edge; ruling 134 made the clock face a non-button.

- **F4** (web console): the Card Away picker opens below the fold with no visible change. A web matter.
- **F5**: above, fixed and re-proved on 1046.
- **F6** (goal and undo pairs on B that no one tapped by intent, 1039, about 09:08 and 09:10): the 09:08 pair follows the
  agent's fixed-coordinate taps straight after the owner reset the density; the 09:10 pair has no agent input in its

  PR at the owner's word (`0925c752`, `PadContent` `scrolls`, set only for a decided board) and checked by the owner on 1035. A break or a tied-no-players card still clips.

## Known limits

- **360 × 560 and below.** The compact tier (smaller score face, one-line names, tighter clock bar) fits 360 × 560 and
  320 × 560 by jsdom; app heights under 556 dp are out of scope (a 360 × 640 phone with 3-button navigation is about
  560), and the Goal square may drop under 48 dp there.
- A clock started by this phone is anchored on the server's clock only when an offset is known: offline after a wall-clock
  jump made while away, the pad reads skewed until the next answer (ruling 141).
- If the app is killed between a late Start's acceptance and its resync, other screens are off by the wait until the next
  clock tap (accepted; rare).
- A team sheet saved after the pad opened shows on the next open: the bootstrap is read once per app process, as for
  cricket.
- The IFAB 2024 extra opponent substitute after a concussion substitute is not modelled (below).

## Follow-ups and parked

For the owner, one line each:

- **Production:** the production device-link answers 404, so Remote Scoring is unavailable on production until the web's
  production deploy. Football on production waits for it.
- **IFAB 2024:** after a concussion substitution the opponent may make one extra substitute. The engine does not model it
  and the phone has no flow; not needed for the 2022 final.
- **Cap counting:** `football.sub` does not count towards the `core.lineup.substitution` cap (a pre-existing gap noted at
  ruling 159). Engine.
- **M6:** an unstamped event does not end a stoppage (parked from the replay review).
- **Web `football.tsx`** is at about 1,600 lines and owes a split under AGENTS' size rule.
- **Web minors m1** (no test of a half-filled extra-time pair at the save door) **and m4** (a ternary's expectation);
  m2 accepted (ruling 165).

  extra-time sub pair, which is visible only in the Match rules form.
- **fr and es rules labels wrap at a space** on device (fr "Exclusion temporaire", es "Tanda de penaltis" and "Expulsión
  temporal") with no mid-word break, while the fit test says one line: the test's metric is looser than the device's.
  Copy ruling 166 (nl "Strafschoppen", fr "Changements") was the one change made.

- **The cricket Undo look:** cricket shows two Undo looks, the shared outlined Undo of ruling 148 and its own (the squares

- The parked minors in the ledger: the sub stale stamp with no floor (M9), two folds per queued tap (M7), five older kit
