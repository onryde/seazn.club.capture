## Findings

1. **The Continue card vanished once, and came back after a rescan.** After a stray navigation (below) Home showed
   the three tiles and no Continue card for the saved scoring match. No Forget was tapped. The phone was killing
   background apps for memory at the time (`OsenseKillAction`, "mem guard"), so a process restart is the likely
   cause, but the log did not say, and it was not reproduced. **Open: unconfirmed.** If a saved match ever drops
   without a Forget, that is a defect in the saved-link store, not in this feature.
2. **A mis-tap, not a defect.** A tap on the language list landed on _Share record_ before the list had finished
   animating and opened Android's share sheet with the session record. Nothing was sent; Back closed it. Driving the
   picker needs a wait before each tap.
3. **The Undo window runs while History is open.** "Undo closes in 41 s" read 24 s on return from History, as the
   45 s window should; opening History does not pause it.
4. **Dutch pad strip is three lines** at 320 dp for the undone-tie-break line (item 5 above). Inside the pad's room
   today; the cost of a longer name is that line, so a name longer than "TEest002ashokashok" is the case to watch.
